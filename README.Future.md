## Proposed Enhancements

- **Graphical User Interface:** Replace text-based UI with graphical marketplace and NFT displays (React + Tailwind CSS).
- **Dynamic Collector Focus:** NPC Collectors shift focus based on market trends.
- **Player-Triggered Events:** Players can initiate market events for strategic advantage.
- **Batch Management:** Market groups NFTs with the same description.
- **TypeScript Integration:** Full type safety across all components.
- **Copyright Lawsuit Event:** Satirical event where a major corporation sues NFT holders for “copyright infringement” over absurd NFTs. Affects prices, can be fought or memed for different outcomes.
- **NFT Fusions:** Players can combine two NFTs to create a hybrid NFT with mixed attributes.
- **Seasonal Events:** Special events for holidays with unique NFTs and market effects.
- **NFT Lending & Renting:** Players can lend or rent NFTs for a fee.
- **Meme Wars:** Meme competitions with rewards and special NFTs.
- **Dynamic NFT Art:** Generative art that changes based on market events or owner actions.
- **DAO Governance:** Players vote on new features/events using NFT holdings.
- **NFT Backgrounds & Expressions:** Add backgrounds/expressions as core attributes with rarity modifiers.
- **Trading Alliances:** Players can form alliances for collaborative trading or event strategies.
- **Meme Contest Leaderboard:** Leaderboard for player-created meme contests to boost NFTs.
- **Achievements System:** Rewards for milestones and diverse playstyles.
- **Collector Rivalries:** Competition between Collector NPCs with opposing focuses.
- **Player-NPC Alliances:** Players can “bribe” NPCs to influence their trading behavior.
